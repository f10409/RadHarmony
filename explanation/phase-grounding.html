    MIMIC-CXR label extraction¶

    TAIX-Ray gives each finding a grade from 0 to 4 (0 to 3 for heart size). Some findings also have a grade for each side (left and right). MIMIC-CXR-JPG gives only the CheXpert codes (1, 0, -1 or blank), with no grade and no side. These codes cannot become TAIX-Ray grades.

    But the free-text MIMIC-CXR reports often say how large a finding is and on which side. So a script reads the reports and finds the grade and the side with regular expressions. It runs on the local computer, with no external API, so no report leaves the computer.

    The script is extract_mimic_labels.py in the separate phase_grounding project. It is not in this repo. This page explains what it does and where it can be wrong. The numbers on this page come from its output file mimic_taix_labels.parquet, read on 2026-11-04.

    Output¶

    The output has one row for each MIMIC-CXR study (version 2.1.0 reports). The script skips a study with no report file. It has 13 columns:

    Column Values
    subject_id, study_id The MIMIC-CXR IDs, as integers
    atelectasis, pleural_effusion, pulmonary_opacities The grade of the finding: the larger of the left and right grades
    atelectasis_left, atelectasis_right, pleural_effusion_left, pleural_effusion_right, pulmonary_opacities_left, pulmonary_opacities_right The grade on one side
    heart_size, pulmonary_congestion The grade of the finding. These findings have no side

    The grades:

    Grade Words in the sentence
    0 no, without, absent, resolved, clear, free of, no evidence of, normal, unremarkable, within normal, stable, unchanged, not enlarged
    1 trace, minimal, tiny, subtle, subcentimeter, micro, small
    2 mild, slight, early, low-grade, minor. Also the default when the sentence has no grade word
    3 moderate, moderate-sized
    4 large, severe, massive, extensive, significant, marked, considerable
    NaN The report does not mention the finding

    The TAIX-Ray heart_size has the grades 0 to 3 (TAIX-Ray). The script gives heart_size the grades 0 to 4, so grade 4 has no TAIX-Ray match.

    How a report becomes grades¶

    The script uses the FINDINGS section of the report. With no FINDINGS section, it uses the IMPRESSION section. With neither, it uses the full text. It splits the text into sentences at each ., ; and new line.

    A sentence mentions a finding when it has one of the words of that finding:

    Finding Words (and their forms)
    atelectasis atelectasis, atelectatic
    pleural_effusion pleural effusion, pleural fluid, hydrothorax, effusion
    pulmonary_opacities opacity, infiltrate, consolidation, airspace disease
    heart_size cardiomegaly, enlarged heart, cardiac enlargement, enlargement of the heart, heart size, cardiac silhouette, "heart is ... enlarged"
    pulmonary_congestion pulmonary edema, interstitial edema, vascular congestion, vascular engorgement, vascular plethora, "vascular markings ... prominent"

    For each sentence that mentions the finding, the script finds every grade word of the sentence. When the sentence has a grade word above 0, the highest such grade wins. A word of grade 0 counts only when the sentence has no grade word above 0. A sentence with no grade word gives 2. Over all sentences of the report, the highest grade wins.

    The side of a lateral finding (atelectasis, pleural effusion, opacities) comes from the same sentence:

    • "bilateral", "bibasilar", "bibasal", "basal", "both lung" or "both side" give both sides. The plurals "both lungs" and "both sides" do not match.
    • "left" and "right" in one sentence give both sides.
    • Only "left" gives the left side. Only "right" gives the right side.
    • A sentence with no side word gives both sides.

    Examples, from a run of the script on 2026-11-04:

    Sentence Output
    "Small bilateral pleural effusions." pleural_effusion 1, left 1, right 1
    "IMPRESSION: Moderate pulmonary edema." pulmonary_congestion 3
    "Heart size is normal." heart_size 0
    "No pleural effusion. Small right pleural effusion." pleural_effusion 1, left 0, right 1

    What the output looks like¶

    The percent of all studies that mention each finding:

    Finding Studies with a grade
    pleural_effusion 67%
    pulmonary_opacities 46%
    heart_size 37%
    atelectasis 25%
    pulmonary_congestion 24%

    Of the studies that mention atelectasis, 7% have grade 0, 9% grade 1, 78% grade 2, 3% grade 3 and 3% grade 4. In 73% of these studies, the left and right grades are the same. 15% have a grade on the left side only, and 10% on the right side only.

    Where the output can be wrong¶

    • Grade 2 is too common. A finding with no grade word gets 2, for example "Atelectasis is present". So 78% of the atelectasis grades are 2.
    • The side is often a guess. A sentence with no side word gives the grade to both sides. A sentence with both "left" and "right" gives each finding both sides. Example: "Left lower lobe opacity and right pleural effusion." gives both sides to the opacity and to the effusion. "Basal" gives both sides, so "Mild basal atelectasis on the left." gives both sides. "Opacities in both lungs, worse on the left." gives the left side only.
    • A negation covers the full sentence, not one finding. A grade word above 0 wins over a negation. Example: "No pleural effusion but mild atelectasis." gives pleural_effusion 2, not 0, because "mild" (grade 2) is in the same sentence.
    • A FINDINGS section ends at the first header word, also inside a sentence and in lower case. The header words are FINDINGS, IMPRESSION, TECHNIQUE, COMPARISON, INDICATION, EXAMINATION, HISTORY and PROCEDURE. Example: the report "FINDINGS: In comparison with the study of ___, there is a new large left pleural effusion." gives the section "In". So the report gets no grade.
    • The word "effusion" alone counts as a pleural effusion. Example: "Small pericardial effusion." gives pleural_effusion 1 on both sides.
    • "Prominent vascular markings." gives no grade, because the pattern needs "vascular marking" followed by "prominent", or the singular "prominent vascular marking".
    • Grade 1 has another meaning than in TAIX-Ray. In TAIX-Ray, grade 1 is "Questionable". Here, grade 1 is a small finding, for example "small effusion".
    • "Stable" gives 0. Example: "Stable cardiomegaly." gives heart_size 0. The project decision D-44 says that a stable finding is present, with grade 2. The script does not follow D-44 yet.

    Use the grades with RadHarmony¶

    subject_id and study_id match the patient_id and study_id of the MIMIC-CXR-JPG harmonized table, after you change the IDs from integers to text. This block adds the grades to the table of the official train split. The prefix taix_ keeps the grade columns apart from the CheXpert label columns with the same names, for example pleural_effusion.

    import pandas as pd
from radharmony.dataset import MIMICCXRJPGTrainDataset

ds = MIMICCXRJPGTrainDataset(base_image_dir="/data/mimic-cxr-jpg/2.0.0/files/", output_cls=True)
df = ds.get_harmonized_df()

grades = pd.read_parquet("/data/phase_grounding/mimic_taix_labels.parquet")
grades = grades.astype({"subject_id": str, "study_id": str}).rename(columns={"subject_id": "patient_id"})
grades = grades.set_index(["patient_id", "study_id"]).add_prefix("taix_").reset_index()
merged = df.merge(grades, on=["patient_id", "study_id"], how="left")
print(len(merged) == len(df), merged["taix_pleural_effusion"].notna().mean().round(2))  # True 0.7

    The grades are extra columns of the table. A dataset class made with harmonized_df=merged keeps the 14 CheXpert labels in cls, because it takes its label columns from its harmonizer.

    The phase_grounding project also has a Gradio app, eval_app.py, on port 7861. Its tabs run the script on a report that you paste, and show the rows of the output file.
